Frequently asked questions

Are the lessons really free?

Yes. All 12 lessons, quizzes and the live editor are free for everyone. You only pay if you want a certificate or personal 1-on-1 sessions.

How do I get a certificate?

Complete all 12 lessons and pass every quiz, publish your final project, then request your certificate on this page. We review your project, you pay the certificate fee, and we send you a certificate with a verification ID.

How can someone check my certificate is real?

Every certificate has a unique ID. Anyone can enter it in the Verify a certificate box on this page to confirm your name and the date.

Do I need any experience?

No. The lessons start from zero and go step by step.

What do I need to learn?

A phone or computer with internet. You can practise right here in the live editor, with nothing to install.

How are the classes held?

Live online on Google Meet or WhatsApp video. We agree a time that suits you when you book.

How do I pay?

See “How to pay” in the tutoring section above. Book on WhatsApp first, then pay, then send us your proof of payment.

What if I need to cancel or I am not satisfied?

You can reschedule a session if you tell us at least 12 hours before it starts. If you are not happy after your first paid session, tell us within 24 hours and we will refund the sessions you have not used.

How does the free promo work?

The first 5 students get one free 1-hour live session. Claim your spot with the form above. Spots are given in the order we receive messages.

How long will it take me to learn?

Go at your own pace. Many beginners finish the 12 lessons in a few weeks, especially with live sessions to ask questions.
